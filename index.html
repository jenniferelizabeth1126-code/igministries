Biblical Counseling

Scripture applied to what you are facing right now.

Biblical counseling provides Scripture-based guidance for a present decision, relationship, transition, spiritual concern, or difficult season.

We look honestly at the situation, bring biblical truth to bear on it, and identify practical next steps you can actually take. The goal is clarity and steadiness, not a quick answer.

How it works

  • Private, focused sessions
  • One conversation about a single decision, or an ongoing rhythm
  • Never locked into a set number of meetings
  • Clear, practical next steps every time
  • Scheduled at a time that works for you

Meet the Founder

Hi, I’m Jennifer Jones.

Prophetic teacher, mentor, coach, entrepreneur, and ministry founder helping people know God, hear His voice, heal, and walk boldly in their calling.

My ministry journey began in 2012 with Watch and Pray, a weekly women’s prayer call created to help women grow in prayer and intercession. From there, God expanded the work into leading intercessory teams and teaching on intimacy with the Holy Spirit, hearing His voice, the prophetic, spiritual disciplines, and keeping a consistent fire for the Lord.

Since 2015, I have counseled, coached, discipled, and mentored men and women in their spiritual growth, personal development, leadership, and purpose. My background spans corporate America, hospitality and events, entrepreneurship, consulting, and church leadership, so I bridge the marketplace and ministry and make biblical truth practical for everyday life.

I disciple others the way I once desired to be mentored: with encouragement, honesty, biblical truth, prayer, and room to grow. My heart is not to create dependence on me or this ministry, but to help you know God for yourself, find your place in the Body of Christ, and run your race with courage.

My prayer is that the fire God lights in you keeps burning, and that you carry it into your home, your work, your community, and the nations. Jennifer Jones
