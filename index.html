Our Mission

Awakened. Equipped. Sent. Illuminate.

Our heart is to make mature disciples who know God, have an intimate relationship with Him and His Word, recognize His voice, steward their spiritual gifts, and live with the courage and boldness to run their race.

We equip people to grow in Christ, receive healing and freedom, understand their calling, serve others, and carry the light of Jesus into their families, communities, workplaces, ministries, and the nations.

Then we send them to reproduce what they have received by strengthening others, making disciples, fanning into flame the gifts God has placed within them, and helping keep the fire of devotion burning from one life to another.

To illuminate is to carry the light of Christ and ignite faith, freedom, and purpose in others.

About the Ministry

Discipleship is life together.

Illuminate Global Ministries is an online discipleship and mentorship community where people are known, supported, challenged, healed, and equipped to walk with God for themselves.

We believe discipleship is more than receiving information. It is learning together, praying together, asking honest questions, applying the Word, receiving mentorship, confronting unhealthy patterns, and allowing God to bring healing and freedom into every area of life.

Our community gives people a place to do life together through live discipleship gatherings, prayer calls, mentorship, teaching, book discussions, and personal connection. We also meet in person for deeper fellowship, worship, prayer, teaching, and ministry.

We are one Body. We honor one another, love one another, and respect the part God has given each person to play. Each person matters, every gift has purpose, and we grow as every part does its work.

You do not have to grow, heal, or pursue freedom alone.

Ephesians 4 describes leaders equipping God’s people for service so the whole Body is built up, reaches maturity, and grows as each part fulfills its role.

Meet the Founder

Equipping people to become who God created them to be.

Jennifer Jones is a prophetic teacher, mentor, coach, entrepreneur, consultant, marketplace leader, and ministry founder who helps people deepen their relationship with God, recognize His voice, understand their identity, receive healing, and move confidently in their God-given calling.

Her ministry journey began in 2012 with Watch and Pray, a weekly women’s prayer call created to help women grow in prayer and intercession. From there, her work expanded to leading intercessory teams and teaching others about intimacy with the Holy Spirit, hearing the voice of God, the prophetic, spiritual disciplines, and maintaining a consistent fire for the Lord.

Since 2015, Jennifer has counseled, coached, discipled, and mentored both men and women in their spiritual growth, personal development, leadership, and purpose. Her experience spans corporate America, entrepreneurship, consulting, and church leadership, allowing her to bridge the marketplace and ministry while making biblical truth practical for everyday life.

Jennifer disciples and mentors others in the way she once desired to be mentored: with encouragement, honesty, biblical truth, prayer, and room to grow. Her heart is not to create dependence upon herself or the ministry, but to help people know God for themselves, discover their place in the Body of Christ, and confidently run the race He has given them.

Through Illuminate Global Ministries, Jennifer desires to ignite the fire of God in others, help them keep that fire burning, and equip them to carry it into their homes, workplaces, businesses, ministries, communities, and nations. Her prayer is that those she serves will become mature disciples who strengthen others and continue making disciples wherever God sends them.

Illuminate Global Ministries is an online discipleship and mentorship community that helps people know God intimately, become rooted in His Word, receive inner healing and freedom, mature spiritually, and become who He created them to be.

Inner Healing and Deliverance

We believe discipleship reaches the whole person.

Biblical teaching helps us understand truth, while inner healing and deliverance address wounds, destructive beliefs, spiritual oppression, recurring cycles, and areas of bondage that may interfere with spiritual growth.

Our goal is not simply to help people know more. We want to see people healed, free, spiritually mature, and able to walk in obedience.

Inner healing is prayer ministry that invites Jesus into places of emotional pain, rejection, trauma, loss, destructive beliefs, and unhealthy patterns.

The goal is to receive healing, replace lies with biblical truth, renew the mind, and move forward with greater freedom and wholeness.

Deliverance is biblical prayer ministry focused on setting the oppressed free through the authority of Jesus Christ.

It may address spiritual oppression, bondage, destructive agreements, recurring cycles, and other spiritual barriers that have affected a person’s growth and freedom.

Sessions are private, prayer-led, and generally last approximately 3 to 4 hours.

Depending on the individual’s needs, a session may include:

  • Forgiveness and repentance
  • Renouncing lies, agreements, and destructive patterns
  • Inner healing prayer
  • Deliverance from spiritual oppression
  • Renewing the mind through Scripture
  • Prayer for continued healing and spiritual growth
